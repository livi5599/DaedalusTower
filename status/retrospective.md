**1\. What went well?**

We all have a lot of motivation to make a high quality game.  Our idea creation and prototyping has been successful, which made it easier to make a solid foundation for next semester.  We have been doing well with meeting once a week for progress reports and to discuss the next steps.  Our meetings with our advisor have been very beneficial, as well as the Unity crash course our advisor has started for us.

**2\. What didn’t go well?**

The main issue was our lack of communication.  There was a decent amount of miscommunication, which made it hard for everyone to always be on the same page.  This made it difficult to have a solid understanding of what the final product should look like, which made it hard to make progress with modeling and visuals.  In addition to these things, we all had busy workloads this semester, so the project was not our main priority; not much physical work was done, which means we will have more to do next semester than we were hoping for.

**3\. What have we learned?**

We learned that our original goals were far too ambitious and that games require a lot of background work and planning before progress can be made on the game itself, so we compressed them so the project is actually doable in the time we have to complete it.  We learned that it is important to bring up comments, questions, and concerns to the group early on to prevent future issues from occurring.  We learned more about puzzle design, basic prototyping pipeline, how game developer workflow is organized, and additional tools that can be used in Unity to make things easier to work with/implement.

**4\. What are still issues/problems/puzzles?**

Getting everything combined into a final product, collaboration/communication, and implementing puzzles from scratch.

**Recommendations for Senior Design 1 next fall:**  
**1\. What went well and worked and should be continued?**

The structure of the class was nice, as these assignments need ideas, genuine thought, and feedback.  The classes also gave practice in public speaking.  Senior Design 1 gives students the opportunity to pursue their interests with like minded individuals, as well as the tools and credibility to find internships/jobs.

**2\. What didn’t go so well and should be changed?**

Since the classes weren’t consistent every week and only were once to twice a month, it made it hard to focus on the class and keep it a high priority.  More information/explanation on how detailed assignments need to be would be nice, and help with group workflow would also be beneficial since a lot of students haven’t had experience in making a big project based mainly on their own decisions.
